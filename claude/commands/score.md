---
description: Plan an eks score, or create one
argument-hint: "[path/to/score.py[::name]]"
---

Load the eks-harness:eks-scores skill. With a path in $ARGUMENTS, run `eks-harness plan $ARGUMENTS --json` and explain the timeline: the clock (song, window, tempo), the takes with their pinned steps and times, the embedded scenes, the edit's tracks and the checks, plus every validation issue with its source line. Say plainly that takes, Blender scenes and live preview do not render under `eks-harness run` yet. Without a path, ask what the score should do and write `.harness/scores/<name>.py` with `@eks.score`, then plan it.
